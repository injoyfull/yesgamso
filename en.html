MANIFESTO

How many things did you feel today?

A gust of wind, a passing scent, something your fingertips brushed against. They were right there beside you — and most days, we let them slip by unnoticed.

Nobody switches their senses off on purpose.

It's just that the small choices we make to get through the day set them down, one by one.

The morning a cold blocked your nose and your favourite soup tasted like warm water. The evening you ate in front of a screen and couldn't recall a single flavour afterwards. We tend to notice things only once they're gone.

And the senses that stay awake differ from person to person. Some of us answer first to sound, some to colour, some to what the hands touch. Noticing which senses are sharp in you and which have gone quiet — that is where knowing yourself begins.

To feel alive is to have your senses alive. So we study how to wake the senses of ordinary life. Not through anything grand — through small, everyday experiences.

Pause for a moment Sense for a moment Create for a moment

Creating doesn't have to be impressive. You don't have to be good at it. Enjoying the process is more than enough.

The moment “it was nice, I guess” turns into “cooled wind, the smell of grass, a grey sky” — an ordinary day turns strange again, and starts coming back to life.

ORIGIN

How we came together

We never set out to make grand art. Just a wish that happiness might come not from something enormous, but from feeling your way through an ordinary day. That's where YES:GAMSO started.

We hold down our own work, and we cross each day as somebody's mother and father too. Getting through what's given to us. Raising the children who came to us, even on the days parenting doesn't suit us at all. All of us, in our own way —

we've worked so hard just to keep living.

What does it mean to pat yourself on the back for that? People who had carried this question for a long time ended up in the same room. We know because we've been through it. What's needed isn't a grand consolation, but one switch turned on in today.

YES:GAMSO is a sensory arts recovery project — helping anyone discover the artist in themselves, by waking the senses and expressing them freely.
